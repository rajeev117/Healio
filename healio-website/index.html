How it works

The hand-off, without the clipboard.

The patient's QR is the thread. Staff only ever see work for someone standing in front of them — never a queue of guesses.

1

Book & check in

The patient books in Healio and arrives with a check-in QR on their phone.

2

Consult

The doctor consults and uploads the prescription. That is the whole job — no flags, no routing.

3

Scan

At the lab or pharmacy, staff scan the same QR and pick the visit. The order is created on the spot.

4

Close the loop

Results and dispensing flow straight back to the doctor and the patient, in real time.
